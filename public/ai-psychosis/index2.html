Why this matters beyond one defendant

The issue is not whether courts may evaluate a defendant when genuine evidence raises doubt about competency. They can and must. The issue here is whether persistent self-advocacy, disagreement with appointed counsel, and use of mainstream AI tools can be converted into a basis for psychiatric confinement despite contemporary evidence of organized, functional behavior.

If that theory is accepted, competency procedure can become a mechanism for disabling a defendant's ability to participate in a contested case rather than a safeguard for defendants who genuinely cannot understand or assist in their defense. That is the public-interest question I am asking journalists and lawyers to examine.
